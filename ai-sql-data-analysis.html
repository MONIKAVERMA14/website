AI & DATA ANALYSIS

AI makes analysis faster.
Human judgment makes it useful.

Partnering with AI gives analysts more time to understand the business, shape the final product and check whether the answer is trustworthy.

THE PARTNERSHIP

Faster execution creates room for better thinking.

AI can help write SQL, explore approaches and draft repeatable checks. That efficiency matters most when analysts reinvest the saved time in understanding the use case, checking the data and preparing something people can actually use.

THE HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP PYRAMID

The closer you get to a decision, the more context matters.

AI can support every layer. The analyst remains accountable for the meaning and impact of the work.

ONE QUESTION, TWO WAYS TO WORK

Which marketing channels drove completed-order revenue last month?

The business question stays the same. AI changes where the analyst spends time.

TRADITIONAL WORKFLOW

One analyst, every production step

↻Manual build + debug cycle
  1. Work with stakeholders

  3. Inspect tables and data availability
  4. Write and debug the SQL
  5. Validate results and explain findings
  6. Document and publish

Careful work, but writing and debugging can take time away from interpreting the result.

A PRACTICAL WORKFLOW

Use AI throughout the work, with a clear owner for each decision.

01 · HUMAN LEADS

Frame the business question

Define the use case, the decision it supports, historical patterns and the expected output. Explain what a useful result would change.

02 · AI ASSISTS

Build a first version

Ask AI to draft SQL, outline the analysis or propose a dashboard layout. Give it the metric definitions and data constraints.

03 · AI + HUMAN

Set up checks

Use AI to suggest tests for joins, missing values, duplicate rows, totals and edge cases. Run the checks against trusted sources and investigate failures.

04 · HUMAN LEADS

Interpret and deliver

Compare results with historical performance and business reality. Refine the story and format for the people who will use it.

05 · DOCUMENT AFTER VALIDATION

Make the work reusable

Once the logic and output match the plan, use AI to draft clear documentation: assumptions, definitions, tests performed, limitations and how to refresh the analysis. Review it before sharing.

THE TAKEAWAY

AI can multiply an analyst’s capacity. Business understanding gives that capacity direction.

The strongest analysts know the history behind a metric, understand the use case and can explain how the result will affect a decision. AI helps them move faster; human judgment keeps the final product relevant and reliable.
