AI & THE FUTURE OF ANALYTICS

AI will change analytics work—but it will not remove the need for strong analysts.

AI can accelerate querying, reporting, documentation, summarization, and even parts of analysis. That means analysts can spend less time on repetitive work and more time on business thinking, problem framing, validation, communication, and decision support.

The professionals who will stay valuable are the ones who combine data skills with business understanding, critical thinking, communication, and the ability to use AI responsibly.
