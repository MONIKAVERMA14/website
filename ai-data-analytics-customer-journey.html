THE BIG SHIFT

Building faster is useful. Learning faster is the advantage.

AI can generate code, draft a dashboard or suggest product features in minutes. But a product only becomes valuable when you understand who uses it, where they struggle and what outcome they need. That understanding comes from combining customer context with reliable data.

THE BASICS STILL MATTER

AI is most useful when the foundation is clear.

You do not need to become a data engineer on day one. You do need enough foundation to question the input, validate the output and explain what the result means.

WHERE AI PLUGS IN

Let AI accelerate the work—not replace the thinking.

01Explore

Translate a business question into possible metrics, segments and hypotheses.

02Build

Draft SQL, Python, tracking plans, transformations or dashboard logic.

03Check

Suggest tests for missing data, duplicates, joins, totals and unusual patterns.

04Explain

Summarise findings for different audiences and document the validated logic.

Human review at every stageIs the question right? Is the data reliable? Does the insight fit the business reality? What decision should follow?

EARLY-CAREER ROADMAP

Learn in the order that helps you solve problems.

  1. 1
    Understand the customer journeyLearn funnels, touchpoints, goals and friction.
  2. 2
    Learn data structureRows, columns, tables, keys and relationships.
  3. 3
    Query and visualiseSQL plus Excel, Tableau or Power BI.
  4. 4
    Understand data movementAPIs, pipelines and external-to-internal connections.
  5. 5
    Add AI to the workflowPrompt, generate, test, review and document.

THE TAKEAWAY

The winning skill is not “using AI.” It is using AI with data foundations to solve a customer problem.

Start with the journey. Understand how the data is stored, structured and connected. Then use AI to move faster—while you remain responsible for the question, the validation and the decision.
